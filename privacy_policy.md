# Ebook Delight Privacy Policy

*Effective November 17, 2026 · Ebook Delight for Android, by Tribow*

> **In short**
>
> - Ebook Delight has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
> - Your books, highlights, notes and reading progress stay on your device. We don't have servers and we never receive them.
> - Text only leaves the app when you choose to send it somewhere, for example to translate a line, ask an AI app about it, search for it or share it.
> - Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

## Who we are

Ebook Delight is made by Tribow ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy or your data, email us at [tribowcompany@gmail.com](mailto:tribowcompany@gmail.com).

## Information we collect

We don't collect personal information. Ebook Delight doesn't ask for your name, email address, location, contacts or any other personal details. The app has no sign-up or login, contains no advertising or analytics SDKs, and doesn't send your reading data to us or to anyone else.

## Information stored on your device

To work as a reader, the app keeps the following in its private storage on your device. Other apps can't read it, and it never leaves your device unless you move it yourself (see "Backups" below):

- the books and documents you add (EPUB, PDF, Word, TXT, CBZ, CBR) and their covers;
- pages you scan with the camera;
- your library, shelves, reading progress and bookmarks;
- highlights, notes and the images you attach to notes;
- read-aloud history (which passages you listened to);
- your settings, such as theme, page color, font and language;
- a search index of your books' text, rebuilt on the device as needed.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes all of this. You can also remove individual books, highlights and notes in the app at any time.

## Things you choose to send to other apps

Some features hand text to another app or website that you pick. They only run when you tap them, and once the text is there, that app's or website's own privacy policy applies:

- **Translate:** sends the selected text to a translation app on your device, or to Google Translate in your browser.
- **Ask AI:** sends the selected text, the sentences around it and the book's title, as a prompt, to the AI app you choose (for example ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude) or to the ChatGPT website.
- **Search:** sends the selected text to your web search app or to Google Search in your browser.
- **Share:** sends the text, quote card image or book file to the app you pick in Android's share sheet.
- **Links:** links inside your books open in your browser.
- **Feedback:** "Contact us" opens your email app with our address. We only receive what you decide to send.

## Read aloud

Read aloud uses the text-to-speech engine you've chosen in your Android settings (for example Speech Services by Google). The app passes the text being read to that engine. Depending on the engine and the voice you choose, the engine may process it on your device or online, under its own provider's privacy policy.

## Android Auto

If you connect your phone to Android Auto, the app shows your shelves and plays read-aloud audio on your car's screen and speakers. This happens through Android Auto on your phone. We don't receive any information about your car or your trips.

## Services provided by Google

- **Google Play Billing:** buying Delight Pro goes through Google Play. Google processes your payment and tells the app whether you own Pro. We don't receive your payment information. See the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Document scanner:** scanning paper pages uses the ML Kit document scanner from Google Play services. The pages are processed on your device. As part of Google Play services, ML Kit may send Google limited technical information about how the feature performs, such as device type and error counts, but not your scanned pages. See [ML Kit's terms](https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms).

These Google components are why the app includes the internet permission. Ebook Delight itself doesn't send your data anywhere.

## Backups

- **Google backup:** if Android backup is turned on, only the app's settings are included in your Google backup. Your books, highlights and notes are not.
- **Moving to a new phone:** when you transfer your apps with Android's setup tools, your library moves along with them, directly between your devices.
- **Library backup:** the "Backup & restore" option in Settings creates a file of your library that you save wherever you choose. We never receive it.

## Permissions

- **Notifications, foreground service and wake lock:** show read-aloud playback controls and keep reading while the screen is off.
- **Internet and network state:** used by Google Play Billing and Google Play services, as described above.
- **Run at startup:** keeps home screen widgets up to date after the phone restarts.
- **Vibration:** gentle feedback on some taps.

The app doesn't ask for access to your location, contacts, microphone or your phone's files. Books are added only through the files you pick or share with Ebook Delight.

## Widgets

Home screen widgets can show the book you're reading, a highlight or a shelf. Anyone who can see your home screen can see them, so remove a widget if you'd rather keep it private.

## Children

Ebook Delight isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Your choices and rights

Because we don't hold any personal data about you, there's nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. Everything lives on your device and is under your control. Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR, the UK GDPR or KVKK. If you have any request, email us and we'll help.

## Changes to this policy

If the app starts handling data differently, for example by adding an optional sync feature, we'll update this policy and the date above before that version is released.
